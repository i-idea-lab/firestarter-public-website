Executive Profile

Rayomand J. Patell

Founder & Chief Creative Officer // CCO, MONKEYWAY GmbH

Rayomand (widely known across the industry as "Rayo") is one of India's most respected creative leaders. His career spans over thirty years across Everest Dentsu Young & Rubicam, SSC&B Lintas (during the agency’s historic win of India's first Cannes Gold Lion), over a decade as Vice President & Senior Creative Director at Contract Advertising (WPP), and Executive Creative Director at Havas Worldwide Mumbai.

Over an illustrious 11-year tenure as Vice President and Senior Creative Director at Contract Advertising (WPP), Rayo spearheaded defining campaigns for marquee national brands. He has subsequently led digital innovation as CCO at Idealake, Schbang, and Group CCO at Laqshya Media Group.

A frequent Grand Jury member for premier advertising and design award festivals, Rayo operates Firestarter with an easy-going demeanor, deep respect for craft, and zero tolerance for agency pretense.

Direct Founder Partnership: Clients engage directly with Rayomand on every brief. No intermediate account executives, no junior delegation, no pitch-team bait and switch.
