The Core Philosophy // No Pitch Theater

Firestarter was founded by advertising veteran and classic car restorer Rayomand "Rayo" Patell on a simple, uncompromising thesis: Great work without the bullshit.

For three decades, we have steered brand turnarounds, engineered breakthrough campaigns, and pushed the boundaries from India's first Cannes Gold Lion era to led creative stewardship across Contract Advertising (WPP), Havas, and India's first Cannes Gold Lion team.

Today, Firestarter combines the best of the past (meticulous brand definition, sharp positioning, razor-sharp copy) with the horsepower of the future (real-time 3D streaming powered by MONKEYWAY Munich, AI newsroom production, and high-frequency deployment).
